# Code of conduct

[Русская версия](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.ru.md)

This is a small project. Most of what happens here is one person reading what somebody else
wrote and deciding whether it is right. That is the whole of it, and this document exists so
that nobody has to guess what happens when it goes wrong.

## What is expected

**Argue about the work.** Disagreement is the point of a review, and a change is judged on
whether it is correct, not on who wrote it. "This is wrong, and here is the case that breaks
it" is exactly the contribution this project wants.

**Say what you actually found.** A report that describes a failure honestly — including the
parts that are unflattering to the reporter — is worth more than one that is tidy. Nobody is
expected to have diagnosed a problem before reporting it.

**Assume the other person is trying.** Everyone here is working with incomplete information
about somebody else's machine, and English is not everyone's first language.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, whether in public or in private.
- Personal attacks, insults, or dismissing someone rather than their argument.
- Sexualised language or attention, in any part of the project.
- Publishing anyone's private information — an address, an employer, a real name they have
  not used here — without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption: reopening a settled decision to make a point, or arguing to exhaust
  rather than to convince.

None of this becomes acceptable because the technical point underneath it was correct.

## Where this applies

Everywhere the project is: issues, pull requests, commit messages, code comments, and
anywhere someone is speaking as a participant in it.

## Reporting

Report privately, through the same route as a security problem:
[open a security advisory](https://github.com/MrAliev/local-ai-developer-toolkit/security/advisories/new).
It reaches the maintainer and nobody else, and it means neither party has to publish a
personal email address to have this conversation.

If the report is about the maintainer, or the advisory route is not one you can use, GitHub
accepts reports about a repository's conduct directly at
[github.com/contact/report-abuse](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse). A code of conduct
whose only path runs through the person being reported is not one.

Expect a first reply within a week.

## What happens then

The maintainer reads it, asks what is unclear, and decides. Depending on what happened, that
can mean a private word, an edit or removal of what was written, or losing the ability to take
part in the repository — briefly or for good.

A report is kept private. The person who made it is not named to the person it is about
without asking first, and a report made in good faith costs the reporter nothing even if the
conclusion goes the other way.
